# Community code of conduct

DSH Eval Lab is a technical community for careful, evidence-bound evaluation. Participants are expected to
be respectful, specific and willing to distinguish facts, interpretations, uncertainty and measurement
failure.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, credential
sharing and attempts to smuggle production data into public Capsules are not acceptable. Disagreement about
domain truth or Evaluator design is welcome when it cites sources, applicability and concrete calibration
evidence.

Maintainers may edit, hide or reject contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict repeated
abusive participation. Enforcement decisions should preserve relevant evidence while minimizing exposure of
private material. A public contact and escalation path must be selected before Developer Preview.
