# Failure modes

## Contents

1. Truth invention
2. Lifecycle collapse
3. Interview waste
4. Measurement confusion
5. Candidate leakage

## 1. Truth invention

- Industry practice becomes product policy without owner confirmation.
- Code behavior is treated as intended truth despite conflicting product docs.
- A fluent owner answer is summarized without a persisted source ref.
- Missing observation is converted into Candidate failure.

Keep these proposed, conflicted, unresolved, or observability-gap respectively.

## 2. Lifecycle collapse

- A RequirementChangeSet overwrites the current Contract.
- A delivery pass automatically issues a new Contract version.
- A Claim receives a new ID only because wording changed.
- A retired Claim disappears instead of retaining explicit lineage.

Reject the mutation and require a separate owner-issued Contract transition.

## 3. Interview waste

- Ask a fixed exhaustive questionnaire.
- Ask the owner for facts available in authorized artifacts.
- Re-ask unrelated confirmed truth during delta.
- Optimize decision-packet precision by omitting difficult questions.

Read first, ask at most three related questions, scope through the graph, and retain a missing-question audit.

## 4. Measurement confusion

- Treat readiness green as grader admission.
- Average path/digest/conflict failures into a score.
- Optimize number of confirmed Claims.
- Use semantic praise to offset a deterministic failure.

Keep readiness dimensions separate and preserve hard invalidators.

## 5. Candidate leakage

- Mount the Skill or Domain Pack in Candidate runner.
- Copy owner answers, Gold, mutants, or bucket identity into Candidate workspace.
- Let authoring Session lineage become an evaluated Episode.

Stop immediately. Authoring and Evaluation planes require separate profiles, sessions, tools, and artifact roots.
